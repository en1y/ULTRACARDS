Rules at a glance

Setup

Treseta uses the same 40-card Italian deck. There is no trump suit and no card is turned up.

Turn flow

Each round you must follow the led suit if you can. The highest card of the led suit wins.

Card strength

Within a suit the order is 3, 2, Ace, then King, Knight, Jack, then 7 down to 4.

Card values

  • Ace1
  • 2, 3, 13, 12, 11⅓
  • 4, 5, 6, 70
  • Last round+1

That is 11⅓ points to fight over each hand.

Declarations

In declaration modes you score bonus points for holding sets of Aces, Twos, Threes, or a Napolitana.

Winning

The player or team with more points wins the hand.

Supported modes

  • 2 players, 10 cards each
  • 3 players, 13 cards each
  • 4 players, no teams
  • 4 players, 2 teams
  • Every mode is available with or without declarations
