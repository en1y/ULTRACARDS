How to play Durak

Five short deals, one idea each — attack, defend, throw in, take, and pass the attack on.

Scroll for the full rules ↓

Rules at a glance

Setup

Each player receives six cards. A face-up card beneath the deck marks trump.

Attack

The attacker opens with any card. More cards may be thrown in only when their rank already appears on the table.

Defense

Cover an attack with a higher card of the same suit or any trump. A trump can only be beaten by a higher trump.

Taking and passing

If you cannot or do not want to defend, take the table. When passing is enabled, a matching rank passes the whole attack to the next player.

Turn flow

After a defended bout the table is discarded. Players draw back to six, attackers first and the defender last.

Winning

Once the deck is empty, players who empty their hands are safe. The final player with cards loses as the durak.

Supported modes

  • 2–6 players
  • 24-, 36-, or 54-card packs
  • Optional Jokers, passing, and everyone-may-throw-in rules
