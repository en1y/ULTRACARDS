Rules at a glance

Setup

Briskula uses the 40-card Italian deck. One card is turned face up: its suit is the trump for the whole hand.

Turn flow

Each round, players play one card in turn. You do not have to follow suit.

Which card wins

A trump beats any non-trump. Otherwise the highest card of the led suit wins.

Card values

  • Ace11
  • Three10
  • 134
  • 123
  • 112
  • 2, 4, 5, 6, 70

120 points are in the deck in total.

Winning

Whoever collects more than 60 of the 120 points wins the hand.

Supported modes

  • 2 players, 3 cards in hand
  • 2 players, 4 cards in hand
  • 3 players, no teams
  • 4 players, no teams
  • 4 players, 2 teams
